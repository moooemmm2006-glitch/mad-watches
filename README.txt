MAD WATCHES — IMAGES FOLDER
===========================

Put your product photos in this folder.

1. Copy a photo here, for example:   rolex-submariner.jpg
2. Open js/products.js and change that product's image line to:
      image: "images/rolex-submariner.jpg",
3. Save and refresh the page.

Tips
- Square photos (e.g. 1000 x 1000 px) look best on the product cards.
- Use lowercase file names without spaces (use - instead).
- Keep each photo under ~300 KB so the site stays fast
  (you can compress photos for free at squoosh.app or tinypng.com).

Files already here
- logo.jpg          your logo
- placeholder.svg   shown automatically if a product photo can't load
- favicon.svg       the small icon in the browser tab
