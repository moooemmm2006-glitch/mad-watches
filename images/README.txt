MAD WATCHES — IMAGES FOLDER
===========================

Your logo
- logo-new.jpg        the original logo you uploaded (not loaded by the site: it's 2 MB)
- logo-1200.jpg       a lighter copy, used on the home and about pages
- logo-120.png,
  logo-240.png        the logo with a transparent background (navbar, footer, intro)
- og-image.jpg        the picture shown when the site is shared on WhatsApp / Facebook
- favicon-16.png, favicon-32.png   browser tab icon (the little dial from the "a" in the logo)
- apple-touch-icon.png, icon-192.png, icon-512.png   phone icons (the bracelet watch)
- placeholder.svg     shown automatically if a product photo can't load
- logo.jpg            your previous logo (not used any more, you can delete it)

Product photos
1. Put your photos here, e.g.  presage-1.jpg, presage-2.jpg
2. In js/products.js, write them in the product's images list:
      images: ["images/presage-1.jpg", "images/presage-2.jpg"],
3. Square photos (1200 x 1200 px), lowercase names, no spaces, under ~250 KB
   (compress for free at squoosh.app).

Full guide: docs/ADD-PRODUCTS.md
